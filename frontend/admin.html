Student appOpen Adminer

View and edit student profiles, scores (evidence) and course data. Every save re-processes immediately: a student edit re-plans that student; a knowledge-base edit re-plans everyone. Edits made directly in Adminer are picked up automatically the next time a plan is read (plans are fingerprinted against all their inputs).
