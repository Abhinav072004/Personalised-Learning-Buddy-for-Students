# Student survey kit

> This kit is ready to run. **Results must come from your own institute survey.** Fill in the findings table below and update `docs/product.md` §2 and the personas. Do not submit placeholder numbers.

## Protocol

- Distribute via a Google/Microsoft form to students across branches and years. Target ≥ 60 responses plus 8–10 short interviews.
- **Consent:** the first question asks for consent to use anonymised responses for this project.
- **Anonymisation:** do not collect name, roll number, email or exact CGPA. Use CGPA bands. Strip free-text of names before analysis. Store raw responses only with the team.
- Analyse with simple counts and cross-tabs (branch × goal, year × "discovered electives late").

## Questionnaire

1. Consent to anonymised use (yes/no)
2. Branch; year of study
3. CGPA band (< 6, 6–7, 7–8, 8–9, > 9)
4. What do you want to do after graduation? (software, data/analytics, core job, MS/PhD, GATE/PSU/M.Tech, UPSC/government, MBA/product, startup, not sure, other)
5. How sure are you about this goal? (1–5). Has it changed since joining? (yes/no; from what?)
6. How do you choose electives/minors today? (seniors, faculty advisor, notice board, WhatsApp groups, on my own…)
7. Did you discover a useful elective, minor or NPTEL option too late? (yes/no; which?)
8. Do you know the CGPA cut-off and credit rules for minors / online credit? (yes / partly / no)
9. Hours per week available for learning outside classes (0–3, 3–6, 6–10, 10–15, > 15)
10. Preferred learning formats (short videos, reading, hands-on problems, projects, peer study)
11. Biggest pain points in planning your academics (free text)
12. How do you know you are "ready" for your goal? (grades, projects, mock tests, internships, don't know)
13. Would you use a buddy that builds and adapts a plan for you? (1–5). What would make you trust it?
